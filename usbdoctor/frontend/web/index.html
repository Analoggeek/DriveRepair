What this app can and cannot do

  • Recovers files from deleted, formatted and RAW volumes — working on a copy, never the original.
  • Repairs file-system metadata (boot sectors, FAT mirrors, GPT backups, FSInfo).
  • Detects counterfeit capacity, failing flash and USB link errors from real evidence.
  • Cannot restore physically worn-out NAND or raise a drive's lifespan.
  • Cannot make a dead controller work — that needs hardware recovery.

The 10-step workflow this scan follows
  1. Detect the device and read its identity
  2. Identify the controller, interface and real capacity
  3. Scan metadata and surface for errors
  4. Analyse error codes against the knowledge base
  5. Research the exact model on official sources
  6. Compare repair procedures and pick the safest
  7. Back up / image the device before any write
  8. Apply only verified-safe repairs
  9. Verify the result by re-reading and re-checking
  10. Produce the report

Capacity verification

Writes a pattern file, verifies it by reading it back and always deletes it. This proves whether the drive really stores what it claims — the only reliable way to detect a counterfeit.
